INTERNOT Film Festival

INTERNOT Film Festival “First!”
Friday, January 18, 2013 / 11 pm
The Peoples Improv Theater
123 East 24th Street, New York, NY

Step away from your computer, put on some pants, come down to the PIT Underground and be the “First” to experience the launch of Internot Film Festival.

We will be showcasing 6 short films on the theme of “Firsts” that were developed specifically for our show, and not for the internet, by 6 unique and talented filmmakers. It’s sure to be a fun night of film, and a great opportunity for filmmakers to meet other like-minded folks and have their work seen by a live audience. It’s also a night to celebrate! After the screening the bar will stay open and live music will be playing, so feel free to stay and mingle. Let’s build a fun night of film, and a place for New York filmmakers to call home.

Featuring films by:

Jerstin Crosby (Janks Archive)
Josh Benson
Angel Yau
Greggory Daniels & Mark Bisi
The Grape Dutches of York
Andrew Beckerman

& animated interstitials by Katya Austin

music by Kierstin Gray
