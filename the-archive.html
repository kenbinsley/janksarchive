The Archive

Online Video Archive
From 2012-2026 we hosted an online, searchable video archive of all the janks in our collection. Videos of people telling janks were organized by collection location, date, type (#rippin', #slagging, #albur), and keywords (#mother, #useful, #shit, #mushrooms). This database was searchable and cross-referenceable. In 2027, we took the archive offline, and created a local, searchable database that can be made available upon request.
