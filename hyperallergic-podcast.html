Hyperallergic Podcast

Tania Bruguera, Mariam Ghani, Queens International

Hyperallergic Podcast
30 May, 2016
Hrag Vartanian

Welcome back to the Hyperallergic Podcast. In our latest episode, we continue on our mission to bring you playful, serious, and radical perspectives on art and culture in the world today. This episode focuses on New York’s borough of Queens, which is becoming a growing hub of artistic activity in the city. Tania Bruegera showing her Pangaea tattoo during our interview in Bryant Park. Tania Bruguera showing her Pangaea tattoo during our interview in Bryant Park. We talk to Tania Bruguera about her Immigrant Movement International project in Queens and her experience in Cuba, then we chat with artist Mariam Ghani about her commissioned mural at the Queen Museum, and finally we wander the Queens International biennial with director Laura Raicovich and guest co-curator Lindsey Berfond to discuss the exhibition’s themes of accumulation and globality.
