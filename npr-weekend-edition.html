Your Mama ... Might Be Offended By This Insult Archive

NPR Weekend Edition
24 July, 2016
Hansi Lo Wang

If you love your mom, you might not be too happy with a current exhibition at the Queens Museum in New York City. It's a video installation in which "your mama" jokes and other putdowns are projected onto the walls of a dark room, in a constant loop. The insults were recorded around the world — and, as you can imagine, they aren't very friendly. In one clip, a Philadelphia man taunts, "Your mama's so old she farts dust." In another video, a woman in Belfast, Northern Ireland, barely keeps it together as she quips, "Your mama's so fat she cuts herself and gravy pours out."
